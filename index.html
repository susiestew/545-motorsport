From the Garage to the Grid

I’m Susanna, an engineer chasing a driver’s dream. 545 Motorsport is my journey from the workshop to the racing grid.

Follow the journey

Where it started

My love of motorsport started with engineering. At the University of Glasgow, I spent three years on the Formula Student team, designing and building race cars. Summers were spent as a trainee mechanic in my local garage, and trackside I’ve worked in data analysis in the Britcar championship. Today I work as an engineer while studying motorsport engineering with the National Motorsport Academy.

Now I’m taking everything I’ve learned about the car into the driver’s seat.
