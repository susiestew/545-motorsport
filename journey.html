Road to the grid

The story so far

In October I did my first ever day behind the wheel. I had no prior experience, but I was instantly addicted, and decided to compete in the Javelin Sprint Series the very next day. I finished 6th in my class, in extreme wet conditions.

That sprint was the last of the season, but I’m already looking to next year and where I can go from here. Take a look at my plan below.

The journey

  8. Half season funded
  9. Full season funded
